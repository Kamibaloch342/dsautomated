SECTION 3: COMPREHENSIVE PRODUCT DESCRIPTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ULTRATECH 1321 NOT DRILLED is a modular spill deck configured as a secondary containment platform for one 55-gallon drum. This uncovered, low-profile unit includes an 11 gal sump for collection of leaks, drips, and small spills and supports up to 1,500 lb. Constructed from polyethylene, it is specified for indoor or outdoor use and elevates stored containers above floor level during handling and storage. Key Features: Spill deck design: Low-profile secondary containment platform for 1 drum. Sump capacity: 11 gal. Load rating: Supports up to 1,500 lb. Construction: Polyethylene pallet and polyethylene grate. Dimensions: 25 7/8 in L x 25 7/8 in W x 5 3/4 in H. Configuration: Modular design with no connection holes; uncovered with no cover or doors. Handling features: No forklift pockets; not intended for use with standard forks. Drainage: No drain and no overflow bladder. Color: Yellow pallet with black grate. Regulatory references: EPA 40 CFR 264.175 and SPCC.
