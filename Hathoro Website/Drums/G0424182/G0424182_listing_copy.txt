SECTION 3: COMPREHENSIVE PRODUCT DESCRIPTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Eagle Mfg 1633 is a commercial-grade, modular secondary spill containment platform engineered specifically for the safe storage and dispensing of one 55-gallon industrial drum. Molded from high-density polyethylene (HDPE), this ultra-low-profile containment deck protects warehouse floors, personnel, and surrounding facilities against catastrophic leaks, continuous drips, and corrosive chemical spills.

Key Features: Single-drum capacity (55-gallon); 15-gallon sump capacity; 2,000 lb uniform load rating; 6-1/2 in low profile height; chemical-resistant HDPE deck, grate, and basin; modular U-channel connection strip; fork-truck compatible uncovered design; compliant with EPA 40 CFR 264.175 and SPCC safety standards.
