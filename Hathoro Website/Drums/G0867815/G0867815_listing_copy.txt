SECTION 3: COMPREHENSIVE PRODUCT DESCRIPTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The FILL-RITE FRHP32V is a hand-operated piston drum pump for transferring compatible fluids from 55-gallon drums. It has a polypropylene pump body, piston, adapter, spout, and suction tube, with Viton seals and fluorocarbon/polypropylene wetted materials. This model delivers up to 11 oz. per stroke and is designed for use without electricity. It includes a telescoping suction tube, bung adapter, and spout for drum installation and fluid dispensing. Key Features of the FILL-RITE FRHP32V Hand Operated Drum Pump: Hand-operated piston action: Manual pump operation with no electrical power required. Flow rate: Delivers up to 11 oz. per stroke for low-volume transfer. Drum fit: Designed for 55-gallon drums with a 2 in bung connection and 2 in MNPT bung adapter. Construction: Polypropylene housing and components help resist corrosion in chemical and industrial applications. Compatible fluids: For use with fluids such as antifreeze, automatic transmission fluid, brake fluid, DEF, ethylene glycol, fertilizers, glycerin, motor oil, and windshield washer fluid. DEF compatible: Suitable for diesel exhaust fluid and AUS32 applications. Suction tube: Includes a telescoping polypropylene suction tube adjustable from 19-3/4 in to 35-1/2 in. Connections: 1 in NPT inlet with 3/4 in hose barb discharge connection. Pressure and head: Rated for up to 50 psi max outlet pressure and 6 ft max head. Temperature and fluid range: Operating temperature range of 0 to 120 degrees F, with a pH range of 3.6 to 11.9 and maximum viscosity of 50 SUS. Additional features: Self-priming and able to run dry; includes spout and suction tube, but does not include a discharge hose or nozzle.
