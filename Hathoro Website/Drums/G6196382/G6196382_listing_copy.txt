SECTION 3: COMPREHENSIVE PRODUCT DESCRIPTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The FILL-RITE SD62 is a hand-operated rotary drum pump for transferring fuel and other compatible fluids from a 55-gallon drum. It mounts to a 2 in bung connection and delivers up to 7.5 gallons per 100 revolutions, with no electrical power required. The pump has a cast iron housing with Buna-N seals and comes with a pail spout, telescoping steel suction pipe, no-drip valve, and 1-1/2 in and 2 in NPT bung mounts for installation. Key Features of the FILL-RITE SD62 Hand Operated Drum Pump: Action Type: Rotary hand pump design for manual fluid transfer. Flow Rate: Delivers up to 7.5 gallons per 100 revolutions or 9.6 oz per stroke. Container Fit: Designed for 55-gallon drums with a bung mount connection. Connection Size: 2 in NPT inlet with bung adapter included. Compatible Fluids: Suitable for antifreeze, automatic transmission fluid, biodiesel, brake fluid, diesel fuel, ethylene glycol, glycerin, hydraulic oils, kerosene, motor oils, Stoddard solvent, and windshield washer fluid. Construction: Cast iron housing and adapter with Buna-N seals; aluminum spout and steel suction tube. Suction Tube: Telescoping steel suction pipe adjusts from 13 in to 39 in. Outlet Size: Discharge outlet measures 3/4 in ID and 7/8 in OD. Operating Range: Handles fluid temperatures from 0 to 120 degrees F. Pump Performance: Self-priming design with maximum head of 6 ft and maximum outlet pressure of 50 psi. Included Components: Supplied with pail spout, no-drip valve, telescoping suction pipe, and 1-1/2 in and 2 in NPT bung mounts.
