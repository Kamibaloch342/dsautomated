The CONDOR 1633BDC spill deck is a low-profile secondary spill containment platform configured for use with one 55-gallon drum and rated for 15 gal spill containment capacity. Made of black polyethylene, this uncovered modular unit is intended for indoor use and has a 2,000 lb load capacity.
