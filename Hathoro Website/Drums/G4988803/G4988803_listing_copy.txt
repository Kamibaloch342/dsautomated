SECTION 3: COMPREHENSIVE PRODUCT DESCRIPTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The EAGLE MFG 1633D is a modular spill deck for supporting one 55-gallon drum and includes a 15-gallon sump for secondary containment beneath the container. This uncovered, low-profile unit is made of polyethylene and includes a removable HDPE grating over the containment sump. It is intended for indoor spill control and storage applications involving secondary containment. Key Features: For use with one 55-gallon drum. 15-gallon containment sump capacity. Rated load capacity of up to 2,000 lb. Low-profile design with a height of 6-1/2 in. Polyethylene deck and pallet with removable black HDPE grating. Built-in drain for removal of collected liquid. Modular design connects with ramp models 1689 and 1689B; sump connection capability: Yes. Compatible with standard forks; no forklift pockets. Dimensions: 26-1/4 in L x 26-1/4 in W x 6-1/2 in H. Yellow pallet with black grate. Meets EPA 40 CFR 264.175 and SPCC standards.
